# Standalone cabinet history codebook

The object is contemporaneous formal personal affiliation among qualifying Ministry/Minister-of-State officeholders. The daily calendar is `[2015-01-01, 2026-03-20)`: 4,096 Brasília civil dates, including March 19, 2026. This candidate is incomplete whenever `metadata.json.complete` is false. A definite core is not a complete cabinet set.

`services.csv` separates officeholding from `affiliations.csv`. Office status is dated in `offices.csv`; the president, vice-president and incoming transition teams do not contribute by those roles. A titular remains during an ordinary travel, leave or administrative delegation unless evidence establishes a formal departure or actual interruption. A designated substitute counts when the qualifying office is genuinely vacant. Formal parliamentary returns/reappointments are retained. A signature alone does not remove a continuing titular. New offices awaiting their first minister and SRI functions transferred to the vice-presidency are explicitly adjudicated vacancy states; they are not unidentified ministers.

Dates use a daily closing-state projection and half-open endpoints. Use an explicit effective date or documented actual assumption/possession; where no different effective date is established, use publication of the formal act. Announcement, request and signature can precede these dates. A later public ceremony does not displace an earlier documented actual management start (for example Flávia Arruda and Ciro Nogueira). Source text, raw endpoints, date status and decisions preserve those distinctions. Bounded changes use the latest admissible event date after targeted research; it is an adjudication, not an exact observation. Move adjacent before/after states together. The cutoff clips continuing service without inventing dismissal.

Personal states are `PARTY`, `UNAFFILIATED`, or `UNKNOWN`. A credible dated personal affiliation observation supports forward continuity after a targeted search for changes and contrary evidence, equally for affiliated and unaffiliated people. Historical cabinet tables can explicitly describe a past service interval; that is different from projecting an undated current biography backward. Blank cells remain missing. C026 interprets a deliberately populated dash only in the inspected pinned tables, where known non-affiliated comparators and separate genuinely blank fields establish the convention; its weaker source-notation basis remains visible. Employment by a party, portfolio sponsorship, government support, a technical career and military service are not personal affiliation evidence. Conflicting positive observations and retrospective labels are adjudicated in `decisions.csv`, with credible alternatives retained.

Party identity is stable through renames: PL/PR, MDB/PMDB, Republicanos/PRB, Cidadania/PPS and Podemos/PTN. `party_names.csv` supplies dated display labels; `organizational_events.csv` records renames, mergers and incorporations. Renames do not transfer membership to a different stable identity. DEM and PSL merge on February 8, 2022. A merger is applied only to a person's continuing predecessor affiliation; an already departed member is not transferred. Equal acronyms with a full-name change, such as PP, do not create a party-set transition. A genuine dated displayed-label change is retained and distinguished from a membership change.

Atlas source rows are preserved positionally because minister and administration date columns repeat their headings. `minister_party_raw` and `portfolio_party_raw` are different assertions; only the former is a candidate personal affiliation observation. Inherited documentation interprets `sfp` as no personal affiliation and `ind` as an independent portfolio assignment. Neither substitutes for person/date adjudication when contradicted. Atlas's 34 blank Lula exit fields reflect an incomplete entry-only snapshot (last entry September 13, 2023), not continuous service to 2026. Its NT53 cabinet-party concept differs from the personal-membership object. Available methodological excerpts and the older handoff were inspected; the full NT53 download remained inaccessible (403/401), so no unexamined methodological detail is claimed as verified. See `research/completion/SOURCE_LINEAGE.md`.

Outputs:

- `periods.csv` and `membership.csv`: maximal adjacent equal primary sets and dated labels within each administration. Unidentified calendar segments have no exhaustive primary membership rows. Evidence-quality and witness changes do not split an established set. Genuine returns remain separate episodes.
- `witnesses.csv`: dated person/service/affiliation intersections whose union covers every recorded period-party membership. `atomic_events.csv` preserves changes beneath coalesced periods. `daily_coverage.csv` contains exactly one row per observation date.
- `officeholder_events.csv` and `transitions.csv`: personal/office/organization events and separately classified party-set boundaries. Unidentified boundaries cannot assert entering/leaving parties. Administration boundaries remain explicit even when membership comparison is blocked.
- `office_accounting.csv`: every qualifying office lifespan covered by a holder or explicit vacancy/unknown-occupancy state. `roster_reconciliation.csv` joins prior, Atlas and pinned candidates while retaining raw assertions and excluding only explicit non-entry/out-of-scope records.
- `historical_comparison.csv`: compares the recovered previous continuous chronology, with raw prior labels retained and stable aliases used only as organization identifiers. It distinguishes membership correction, historical party recognition, labels, office scope and dates. It does not use before/after election-year translated columns. `unidentified_interval_resolutions.csv` instead compares the rejected standalone release, and `previous_dependency_resolutions.csv` details each original dependency.
- `residual_person_uncertainty.csv`, `date_sensitivity.csv`, and their input constraints: preserve finite personal and date alternatives and their actual effect. Other officeholders can mask uncertainty only if all supported alternatives give the same union. An officeholder cannot mask their own uncertain party. Unspecified affiliation remains a completion blocker.
- `completion_blockers.csv` and `completion_research_register.csv`: exact remaining dependencies, dates and sources consulted. `completion_case_review.csv` revisits the 30 inherited R cases; it is not falsely presented as the unavailable newer `case_change_dimensions.csv`.
- `evidence.csv`, `decisions.csv`, `evidence_snapshot_links.csv`, retained raw research and the package manifest: source assertions, adjudications, retrieval limitations and hashes. A search extract is not labelled a retrieved original or independent corroboration.

Builds use Python's standard library and local inputs only. No manuscript, electoral or ideological data are read or generated. Third-party attribution is retained; no new license is claimed over source materials.

V5 comparison exports: `v4_daily_coverage.csv` preserves the verified v4 daily baseline as a comparison input. `v4_daily_comparison.csv` distinguishes newly resolved missing days from changes to previously established primary sets; `v4_interval_resolutions.csv` accounts for all 100 v4 unidentified days. `refinement_outcomes.csv` records the targeted case dispositions, and `refinement_validation.csv` records release checks. These additions do not change the historical object, officeholding rule, or interpretation of affiliation states.

A bounded affiliation transition is one jointly moved event, not a free choice of affiliation throughout both adjoining spells. In `residual_person_uncertainty.csv`, records with `alternative_scope=linked_date_window` are clipped to the linked constraint’s `[earliest_effective_date,latest_effective_date)` uncertainty window and actual service. `date_constraint_id` identifies that relationship. Before the earliest and from the latest admissible date the corresponding before/after state is fixed under the adjudication. Independent membership alternatives retain `service_affiliation_intersection` scope. V4’s residual export ignored this scope and overstated affected spans; v5 corrects the export without changing primary states or the source alternatives.


V5 schema 3 separates historical evidence from the user-authorized complete primary export. `primary_assumptions.csv` is a table of `PROVISIONAL_MODEL_ASSUMPTION` records, each linked to an unchanged UNKNOWN affiliation and exact dates. The selected `assumed_state=UNAFFILIATED` contributes no additional party; it is not an observed non-affiliation, an employer-based inference, or a bound on actual alternatives. Every party included in a provisional set still requires the same actual officeholder/affiliation witnesses as before.

Daily `composition_status=primary_provisional` identifies exact assumed dates; `primary_assumption_ids`, `provisional_person_ids`, and the retained `unresolved_dependencies` provide traceability. `unfilled_primary_dependencies` instead measures gaps that still prevent even a provisional primary set. Primary periods coalesce whenever their party sets and dated labels agree, regardless of evidence status; a period containing any provisional dates is labelled `primary_provisional`, with its `provisional_days` and assumption IDs disclosed. This does not label every day within that period as provisional: use daily/atomic rows for exact dates. Transitions touching provisional dates carry assumption IDs and `primary_sets_comparable_provisional` comparison status.

`metadata.identified_days`, `unidentified_days`, and `complete` retain their historical meaning. New `primary_covered_days`, `primary_uncovered_days`, `provisional_days`, and `primary_complete` describe the fully populated primary export. `--require-complete` still rejects unresolved historical evidence; `--require-primary-coverage` separately verifies zero unfilled primary dates. Blocker rows remain historical blockers even after their primary sets are filled; `primary_treatment` and `primary_set_filled` state the operational disposition. Earlier `days_still_blocked` and sensitivity concurrent-unidentified counts likewise concern historical uncertainty, while `primary_unfilled_days` distinguishes any remaining export gap. Imputation is not counted as historical resolution in older comparison outputs.
